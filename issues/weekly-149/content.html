New Podcast Episode: From a Research Scientist at Amazon to a Machine learning/AI Consultant
We've just released a fresh podcast episode featuring Verena Weber, an expert in Natural Language Processing (NLP) with more than seven years of experience in Machine Learning. Verena, previously a Research Scientist at Alexa AI (Amazon), has transitioned to freelancing.

She is a firm advocate for the transformative potential of Generative AI (GenAI) in reshaping our work and interactions with technology. Verena is dedicated to guiding companies in adapting to this imminent change.

We discussed:

Amazon
  • Getting hired at Amazon without a Ph.D.
  • Research at Amazon
  • What it means to be a leading scientist on a project at Amazon
  • Does having a PhD give you an edge in the industry?
Verena as a freelancer
  • The challenges of being self-employed
  • Why Verena chose generative AI
  • Verena’s pitch deck
  • Finding your audience and identifying their pain points
