New Podcast Episode: LLMs for Everyone
We talked with Meryem Arik, a recovering physicist and the co-founder of TitanML.

TitanML is an NLP development platform that focuses on the deployability of LLMs, allowing businesses to build smaller and cheaper deployments of language models with ease.

We discussed:
  • What is an LLM (generative vs non-generative models)?
  • Why LLMs are important?
  • How Meryem became interested in LLMs?
Applying LLMs
  • Open-source models vs API models
  • How fine-tuning a model helps in LLM use cases
  • How generative models change the landscape of human work
  • Vector databases and LLMs
