Interview: Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) for Search Systems
The speaker, Daniel, will be discussing the following topics:
  • The basic principles of vector embeddings
  • Real-world applications of vector embeddings
  • RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) and LLMs
  • How to integrate vector embeddings into existing infrastructure
Daniel, a former Tech Lead at YouTube, built a Machine Learning infrastructure that facilitates the purchase of more than 10 billion USD worth of YouTube Ads every year. 
 
He is now the CEO of Superlinked, a compute and data engineering framework for turning data into vector embeddings, designed for building high-relevance RAG, Search, Recommender, and Analytics systems.
