New Podcast Episode

We uploaded a podcast with Dânia Meira about accelerating the adoption of AI through diversity.

Dânia is a recognized speaker in the field of data science, machine learning, and AI. She is one of the founding members of the AI Guild, where she empowers companies for MLOps and use case deployment.

A small outline of our talk:

  • Diversity
    • Other types of diversity besides gender

    • Advice for people in underrepresented groups
  • AI Guild
    • AI Guild’s consulting work and community
    • How the AI Guild helps organizations become more diverse
    • How the AI Guild finds women in the fields of AI and data science
