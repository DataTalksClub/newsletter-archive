Book: How Software Fails by Engin Yöyen

Based on real-world case studies, this book reveals the hidden laws governing disasters in complex systems like:

  • How Knight Capital lost $460 million in 45 minutes due to a single misplaced software flag
  • Why the Therac-25 radiation machine killed patients despite passing every safety test.
  • How a 40-kilobyte configuration file crashed 8.5 million computers worldwide, grounding flights and shuttering hospitals across the globe.
