Build a Central ML Team
Central ML teams were first created to build and manage ML platform tools for data science and machine learning professionals at top technology companies, with the goal of providing a unified and standardized experience for ML application development. 

As the field of MLOps matures many are questioning whether this type of team structure is right for their organization.

In this mini-event you’ll learn about: 
  • Forming central ML teams
  • Working across ML use cases and lines of business
  • Driving adoption of internal tools
  • Balancing being proactive versus being reactive
Join us at 9am PT / 12pm ET on September 21st, 2022!
