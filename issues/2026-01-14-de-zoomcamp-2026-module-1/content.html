Self-Paced vs. Live Cohort
All Zoomcamp lectures and materials are pre-recorded, regardless of how you take the course. There are no mandatory live classes.

The difference between self-paced and live cohort is about submissions and certification, not about videos.

In self-paced mode, you can watch all lectures, read the documentation, and follow the course at your own pace, but you cannot submit homework, projects, or receive a certificate.

In the live cohort, the same materials are used, but homework deadlines are active, submission forms are open, peer review is enabled for the final project, and a certificate is available if you meet the requirements.

If you want to submit homework, complete the final project, and earn a certificate, you need to participate in the live cohort.

Homework and Submission Process
The deadline for this homework is 27 January 2026, 00:00 CET.
Each module in the live cohort includes a homework assignment designed to help you apply the concepts from the lectures in practice.

For a homework, you are expected to:
  • Work through the tasks locally on your machine
  • Use code, SQL queries, Docker, Terraform, or other tools introduced in the module
  • Validate your results yourself before submitting
The homework itself is hosted on the course platform and requires you to log in.

To submit a homework:
  • You log in to the course platform
  • You answer the multiple-choice questions directly in the homework form
  • For questions that require code or analysis, you publish your solution in a public GitHub repository (or similar) and submit the link in the form
Each homework has a fixed deadline. After the deadline passes, the submission form closes automatically, and late submissions are not accepted.

Homework submissions are:
  • Only available during the live cohort
  • Automatically graded
  • Count toward the leaderboard (optional)
In self-paced mode, you can still complete the homework for practice, but you will not be able to submit it or receive credit.
