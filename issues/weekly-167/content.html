New Podcast Episode: Inclusive Data Leadership Coaching

We published a new podcast episode with Tereza Iofciu, an experienced data practitioner who worked as a data science manager, data scientist, data engineer, and product manager. She leads a coaching team and teaches data science at neuefische in Hamburg.

We discussed:

  • Teresa's career journey
  • Data leadership coaching
  • Challenges in data leadership
  • Leadership skills development
  • Coaching approach and philosophy
  • Feedback and communication within teams
  • Personal development and self-reflection
