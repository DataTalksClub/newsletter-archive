Podcast
Listen to our new podcast on Preparing for a Data Science Interview with Luke Whipps.

Luke Whipps is a co-founder of Neural.AI – a company specializing in recruiting data scientists and other AI professionals. He has 8+ years of experience working as a recruiter.
During the podcast we talked about:
  • Different aspects of the hiring process
    • Getting ready for the interview process
    • Passing the filter between the candidate and the company
    • Preparing for research and communication
    • Establishing the hiring company’s expectations
  • Preparing for the interview
    • Reviewing the fundamentals
    • Passing the technical part of the interview
    • Depth vs breadth
  • Important notes and details about the process
    • Overly theoretical and mathematical questions in interviews
    • Bombing (failing) in the middle of an interview
    • Applying to different roles within the same company
