LLM Zoomcamp 2025: Final Project
It's time to begin your final project if you want to earn a certificate for the LLM Zoomcamp.

Here's what you need to do:
  • Select a dataset that interests you.
  • Ingest the data into a knowledge base.
  • Implement the retrieval flow.
  • Evaluate the performance of your Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) flow.
  • Create an interface for your application.
  • Collect user feedback and monitor your application.
You have two opportunities to submit your final project, with deadlines on September 16 and October 7.
