Book of the week
The book of this week is "Distributed Machine Learning Patterns" by Yuan Tang.

Yuan is a project lead of Argo and Kubeflow, a maintainer of TensorFlow and XGBoost, and an author of numerous open-source projects.
The book covers:
  • Building scalable ML pipelines with data ingestion, training, and serving.
  • Using Kubernetes, TensorFlow, Kubeflow, and Argo Workflows for ML automation.
  • Making informed trade-offs between different patterns and approaches.
  • Managing and monitoring large-scale machine learning workloads.
It offers practical examples and hands-on projects using real-world tools, making it ideal for developers looking to enhance their machine learning systems.
To participate in the book-of-the-week activity:
  • Go to #book-of-the-week channel
  • Ask as many questions as you’d like
  • The book authors answer questions from Monday to Thursday
  • On Friday, the authors decide who wins free copies of their book (ebooks)

New Podcast Episode: Challenges and Innovations in Search Technologies

We published a new podcast episode with Atita Arora, a seasoned and esteemed professional shaping the landscape of information retrieval systems.

She has a robust background as a committer in Apache OpenNLP and Quepid and has written insightful blogs on using vectors in e-commerce search and an open-source implementation called Chorus.

We discussed:

Atita’s experience

  • Career journey in search and NLP
  • Work at Lucidworks and OpenSource Connections
  • Role at Qdrant and vector databases
Evolution of search
  • Major changes in search technologies
  • Impact of large language models and vector search
  • Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) concept and application in search

Building chatbots with LLMs

  • Integrating chatbots in search
  • Techniques for data ingestion and performance evaluation
  • Importance of human oversight in AI systems
  • Vector databases in machine learning
