LLM Kaggle Competition: One Month to Go
Only one month is left until the end of the LLM Zoomcamp 2024 Competition.

It's a Kaggle competition where you'll solve high school math problems using LLMs.

If you're afraid to participate or have questions, we prepared the materials for you:
