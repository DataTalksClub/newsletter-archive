Logistics

I'll tell you more about it tomorrow, but here's a gist:

  • Each week we'll release a set of pre-recorded video and a homework.
  • At the end of the course you'll need to do a project where you'll put everything you learned in the course in practice.
  • After finishing the course, you'll get a certificate. The only criteria for passing the course is finishing the project. 
  • Homework is optional, but you'll get scores for completing it and there'll be a leaderboard.
  • On Mondays at 17:00 CET we'll have live office hours. You can come to the office hours to ask questions and we'll be happy to answer them.
