Lessons from Building Copilot at Microsoft
Join this podcast to hear​ insights from Vadim, whose path spans from algorithmic ML and MIT research to building Copilot at Microsoft.

​In this conversation, we aim to understand what has genuinely changed with generative AI and what fundamentals remain important.

​We'll discuss assistants as complements to human thinking, why embodiment ("physical AI") may be the next step, and how goal-setting and sharing knowledge shape work that lasts.

Book of the Week: Machine Learning Algorithms in Depth
Join our Book of the Week event to ask your questions directly to the authors! Plus, you get a chance to win a free copy of the book.

Dates: September 8-12
Book: Machine Learning Algorithms in Depth by Vadim Smolyakov
Machine Learning Algorithms in Depth explains dozens of algorithms across a variety of applications, like:
  • Finance
  • Computer vision
  • NLP
Each algorithm is mathematically derived, followed by hands-on Python implementations with insightful code annotations and informative graphics.
