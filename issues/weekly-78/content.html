DVC Extension for VS Code
Iterative just released a new VS Code plugin

With it you can:
  • Run, track and version machine learning experiments
  • Build interactive plots for comparing the performance of different models
  • Keep an eye in real-time on the quality of your models as they train   
  • Fully reproduce your past experiments
  • Version control the data for your experiments
All that in the comfort of your VS Code!
 
Yes, I want it now!
