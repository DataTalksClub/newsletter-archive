Book of the Week

We kick off a new initiative: the book of the week. Each week book authors come to Slack to answer questions about their books. At the end of the week, you can win a free copy of the book. You can read more about it on our website. 

This week, the book of the week is Machine Learning Bookcamp by Alexey Grigorev (i.e. me).


 

  • Go to #book-of-the-week channel
  • Ask as many questions as you’d like
  • The book authors answer questions from Monday till Thursday
  • On Friday, the authors decide who wins free copies of their book
