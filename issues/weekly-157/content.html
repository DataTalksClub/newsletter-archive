Book of the Week
The book of this week is Creating Intelligent Products by Janna Lipenkova. Participate from January 22 to January 25 to ask your questions and talk directly to the author of this book!
"Creating Intelligent Products" is a guide for product managers, team leads, and software product stakeholders. It covers integrating AI into software products, focusing on Generative AI, AI-assisted analytics, and smart automation.

The book offers practical insights for identifying AI opportunities in the market, evaluating AI technologies, and communicating with data scientists and ML engineers. It also delves into designing AI interfaces, and best practices in AI ethics, governance, and risk management.

New Podcast Episode: Bayesian Modeling and Probabilistic Programming

We published a new podcast episode with Rob Zinkov, a machine learning engineer and data scientist. His work covers how to more efficiently specify and train deep generative models and discover a good statistical model for your data.

We discussed:

Rob's Career and Machine Learning Evolution

  • Transition from software engineering to Bayesian modeling
  • Development of machine learning tools
  • Academic and industry experiences

Bayesian Modeling Fundamentals

  • Principles and comparison with frequentist approaches
  • Role of probabilistic programming in Bayesian analysis

Probabilistic Programming Languages and Techniques

  • Distinction between programming languages and libraries
  • The function of samplers in Bayesian modeling

Practical Modeling Applications

  • Encoding dependencies in Bayesian models
  • Case studies on rainfall modeling and other scenarios
