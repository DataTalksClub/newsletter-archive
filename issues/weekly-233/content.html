As part of our LLM Zoomcamp, we decided to organize the Summer 2025 AI Hackathon.

You'll get the opportunity to apply your LLM knowledge and create impactful tools for the DataTalks.Club community during this summer.

MLOps Zoomcamp Competition
We are excited to announce the MLOps Zoomcamp 2025 Competition, where you can put your MLOps skills to the test.

This competition provides an excellent opportunity for Zoomcamp students to practice everything they've learned throughout the course.

To join, you'll need to build and deploy one of two types of projects:
  • A bot designed to convince chat participants that it is a real person
  • A classifier that detects whether a chat participant is a bot or a human.
What you get from participating:
  • A portfolio-worthy project showcasing your end-to-end ML system
  • The top 10 participants will get AWS vouchers worth $50
  • MLOps Zoomcamp students will receive extra course points
