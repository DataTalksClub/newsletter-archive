Book of the Week: Understanding ETL
The book of this week is "Understanding ETL" by Matt Palmer.
If you want to do anything with data, you need a reliable process or pipeline.

ETL (Extract, transform, load) describes most data practitioners' foundational workflows—taking data from a source system, changing it to suit their needs, and loading it to a target.

This book explains the components of ETL, step-by-step, discussing architecture, maintainability, and scalability.
