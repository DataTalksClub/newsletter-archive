DataTalks.Club Wrapped 2025
As the year comes to an end, we want to pause and reflect on what the DataTalks.Club community built together in 2025.

This year, 5,000+ people submitted at least one homework in our free Zoomcamps. That means learning moved beyond watching videos and into practice.

Together, community members:
  • Spent 1.6M+ hours learning, building projects, and experimenting with real tools
  • Earned 132,949 points worth of hands-on work
  • Received 844 certificates along the way
Behind these numbers are thousands of small, everyday efforts: running experiments that didn't work the first time, debugging pipelines late at night, asking questions in Slack, reviewing peers' work, and sharing progress publicly.

Over the course of the year, our Slack community also continued to grow. We started with 66,000 members in January 2025 and are now 83,000+.

To make this collective effort visible, we created DataTalks.Club Wrapped 2025. There, you can explore:
  • Your personal learning Wrapped
  • Course-level metrics
  • Community-wide statistics
Thank you to everyone who learned with us this year!

DataTalks.Club works because people show up and do the work.

As we're heading into the new year, we're launching a survey to better understand how people actually work with AI, data engineering, MLOps, and developer-focused AI tools today.

Your answers will help us build a grounded view of the tooling landscape:
