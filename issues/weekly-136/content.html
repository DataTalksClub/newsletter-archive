🔧 Upcoming Workshop
In this workshop, Matt Palmer, a Developer Success Engineer at Mage, will present a demo of an end-to-end pipeline with Mage, covering:
  • Ingesting data from a streaming source
  • Performing transformations, both in Python and with dbt
  • Writing to a data source
Additionally, the presentation will discuss how Mage fits into the modern data stack and how it can be deployed to infrastructure or used for data engineering/analysis projects.

Data Strategy: Key Principles and Best Practices
We don't have a new podcast episode this week. But here is one of the popular podcasts you can listen to.

In this podcast, we chatted live with Boyan Angelov, a data strategist with a decade of experience in various academic and commercial environments. He authored two books: “Elements of Data Strategy” and “Python and R for the Modern Data Scientist”. Currently, he is leading the data strategy function at Exxeta AG, a large DACH technology and digital transformation consultancy.

We discussed:
  • What is data strategy?
  • Designing a data strategy
  • Data Strategist vs Data Science Strategist
  • The skills one needs to be a data strategist
  • Pitching a data strategy to a stakeholder
  • and more!
