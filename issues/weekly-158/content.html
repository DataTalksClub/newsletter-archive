New Podcast Episode: Machine Learning Engineering in Finance

We published a new podcast episode with Nemanja Radojkovic, a Senior Machine Learning Engineer at Euroclear.

His professional journey spans from being an Electrical Engineer to transitioning into roles as a Data Scientist and MLOps Engineer.

We discussed:

Machine Learning Engineering in Finance
  • Discussion on machine learning engineering in the financial sector
  • Challenges and work nature of ML engineers in finance
  • Use cases and projects within the financial industry
Career Journey and Transitions
  • Guest's career journey from electrical engineering to data science and MLOps
  • Transition from academia to industry
  • Experience in various companies within the financial sector
MLOps and its Integration in Finance
  • MLOps in financial institutions
  • Integration of MLOps with existing DevOps processes
  • Challenges and solutions in implementing MLOps in a regulated industry
Technologies and Soft Skills
  • Adaptation to different tech stacks based on project requirements
  • Importance of soft skills in driving change within organizations
  • Strategies for promoting and implementing new technologies and processes
