Book of the Week
Ask your questions actively on our Slack community and get one of the five free copies of the book!

📕 The book of this week is Hands-on Time Series Analysis with Python by Ashish Patel.
The book covers the latest time series packages like fbprophet and pmdarima.

It introduces readers to a wide range of methods such as Smoothening, ARIMA, SARIMA, SARIMAX, VAR, VARMA, and AUTO-ARIMA.

The authors also explain how to leverage advanced deep learning-based techniques like RNN, LSTM, CNN.
To participate:
  • Go to #book-of-the-week channel
  • Ask as many questions as you’d like
  • The book authors answer questions from Monday to Thursday
  • On Friday, the authors decide who wins free copies of their book (ebooks)
