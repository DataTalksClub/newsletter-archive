New Podcast Episode: From MLOps to DataOps
We chatted with Santona Tuli, who started her career as a research data scientist at CERN, searching through massive event data from particle collisions!

Now at Upsolver, she leads data engineering and science. Santona is passionate about building and empowering others to scalably build end-to-end data and ML pipelines.

We discussed:
  • Focusing on data workflows
  • Upsolver vs DBT
  • ML pipelines vs Data pipelines and tools for it
  • The “modern data stack” and today's data ecosystem
  • Human-centric vs Machine-centric pipeline
  • Applying skills learned in academia to ML engineering
  • Crafting user personas based on real stories
  • A framework of curiosity
