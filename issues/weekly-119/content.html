Podcast about Navigating Industrial Data Challenges
We had an in-depth chat with Rosona Eldred.

Rosona is a trained mathematician working in the “data space” for the last 6 years, the last three with industrial data. She is currently a machine learning engineer with a technical leadership role around synthetic tabular data in an AI Innovation team.

We discussed:

From academia into industry
  • Rosona's background
  • How mathematics knowledge helps in industry
  • The advantages of coming from academia into productive industry
Industrial data
  • What is industrial data?
  • Internet companies’ data vs industrial data
  • How data specialists use industrial data
  • Types of industrial data
Productive industry
  • Why productive industry needs data
  • Tiny data vs Big data in productive industry
