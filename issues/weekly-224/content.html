AI Math Olympiad: A Technical Debrief of the Competition

Join us for a technical webinar with Ilya Boytsov, deep learning research engineer at Wayfair and co-lead of the Oxford LLMs project.

In this talk, Ilya will cover:

  • The math domain in the context of LLMs
  • Post-training strategies to improve LLM accuracy
  • Latency-constrained optimization: model selection, inference frameworks, and runtime trade-offs

Perfect for machine learning engineers and researchers interested in real-world challenges at the intersection of accuracy and performance in LLM systems.
