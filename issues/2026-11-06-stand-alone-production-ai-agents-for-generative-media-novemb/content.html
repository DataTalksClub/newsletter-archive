Production AI Agents for Generative Media: Free Cohort Starts November 16

Production AI Agents for Generative Media is our new free course with Nebius, and it starts with a question: what do you do when your agent can kick off GPU jobs that cost a lot, take a while, and sometimes fail?


You'll figure it out by building a real agent that creates images, voice, and video, and you'll come away knowing when running your own models beats paying for per-call APIs.

Learn more about the course


Over 5 weeks, you build one system from start to finish: a Personalized Media Factory. A brief goes in, an agent plans the asset and writes its text, and GPU models turn it into an image plus voice or video.

Along the way, you learn to:

  • Run image, video, and audio models on serverless GPUs, with no cluster to set up or babysit

  • Turn GPU models into tools your agent can call, and let it redo anything that isn't good enough

  • Scale from one record to 1,000+ on spot GPUs, recover from failures without starting over, and see what every asset costs

  • Add evals, tracing, and SLOs, so you can tell your team what the system costs and when it's ready to ship



The lectures and materials are free for everyone, but the live cohort is by application only, with limited seats. Selected participants get peer-reviewed homework, weekly live office hours, GPU credits through the Nebius AI Builder Program, and a capstone project shared publicly that's ready for your portfolio. The course starts Monday, November 16.
