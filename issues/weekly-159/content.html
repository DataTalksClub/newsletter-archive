Book of the Week
The book of this week is Creating Intelligent Products by Janna Lipenkova. Participate from Feb 5 to Feb 9 to ask your questions and talk directly to the author of this book!

"Creating Intelligent Products" is a guide for product managers, team leads, and software product stakeholders. It covers integrating AI into software products, focusing on Generative AI, AI-assisted analytics, and smart automation.

The book offers practical insights for identifying AI opportunities in the market, evaluating AI technologies, and communicating with data scientists and ML engineers. It also delves into designing AI interfaces, and best practices in AI ethics, governance, and risk management.

Workshop: Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) in Action
Join this free workshop and learn how to build a Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) system using open-source tools.

You'll learn:
  • The fundamentals for data retrieval
  • How to leverage open-source tools for data-centric RAG
  • How to inspect and understand your data
  • How to choose the right embedding model
  • Advanced RAG and other useful applications beyond chatbots
By the end of the workshop, you'll be able to build advanced RAG systems and have a clear understanding of all the applicable use cases for this amazing technology.
