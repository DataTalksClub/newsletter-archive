Open Source Spotlight

Open-Source Spotlight is our series where we're discovering open-source tools. We talk with the creators of these tools and record a short demo to show you how to work with the tool.

This time, we talked about LineaPy. LineaPy helps to capture, analyze, and transform messy notebooks into data pipelines with just two lines of code.
