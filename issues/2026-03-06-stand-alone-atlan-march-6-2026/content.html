Uncomfortable Truth about Most AI Agent Deployments: They're Guessing

Someone asks an agent, "What are our top 10 shows?" and the agent doesn't know if "top" means views or watch time, if "new" means released this month or added to the platform, or which table even holds the answer. Different teams get different numbers. Nobody trusts any of them.

The problem isn't the model. It's the context.

On March 11th, we'll show you how to fix it live. 

You'll see how Atlan's Context Studio and Snowflake's OSI (Open Semantic Interchange) - the standard that makes context portable, consistent, and usable by any AI tool in your stack - close the context gap that's keeping AI analysts stuck in pilot and out of production.

1) Why context is the real bottleneck—and how to solve it

Watch context flow from Context Studio through OSI to Snowflake Cortex, Claude, and custom agents—no rebuilding required. One context layer, infinite interfaces.
