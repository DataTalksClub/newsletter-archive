From devices to people: how Autotrader found its high-intent shoppers

Autotrader, the UK’s largest automotive marketplace, couldn’t always tell that the person browsing on a laptop was the same one calling a dealer from their phone. A new case study shows how Snowplow Identities closed that gap.

Watch the Session

Autotrader’s personalization engine works in real time, but its identity stitch ran as a daily batch job, so new links between devices took hours to arrive. After a month-long proof of concept against an in-house streaming build, the team moved identity resolution into their existing Snowplow pipeline.


Autotrader showcases:

  • How Autotrader ran a build-versus-buy test, and how the two options compared on speed, complexity and cost

  • Why real-time graph traversal in Spark Streaming took thousands of lines of code

  • How Snowplow Identities links devices to one person inside the pipeline, and merges profiles when a new link appears

  • Why only around 4% of visitors could be stitched, yet stitching made the biggest difference for shoppers about to contact a dealer


The result: identity processing went from a daily batch job to milliseconds, at about the same cost as the in-house option once engineering time was counted. Read the case study for the full numbers.
