Book of the Week
Ask your questions actively on our Slack community and get one of the five free copies of the book!

📕 The book of this week is "Prompt Engineering for Generative AI" by Michael Taylor and James Phoenix.
Gain a solid understanding of generative AI with practical applications. This book covers:
  • The structure of the interaction chain of your program's AI model and the detailed steps involved
  • How AI model requests are generated by transforming the application problem into a document completion problem in the model training domain
  • The impact of LLM and diffusion model architecture, and the best methods for interacting with them
  • How these principles are applied in natural language processing, text and image generation, and code domains

To participate:

  • Go to the #book-of-the-week channel in our Slack
  • Ask as many questions as you’d like
  • The book authors answer questions from Monday to Thursday
  • On Friday, the authors will choose the winners of their free ebook copies by selecting the best questions

New Podcast Episode: Working as a Core Developer in the Scikit-Learn Universe

We published a new podcast episode with Guillaume Lemaître, an open-source software engineer at :probabl. and a core maintainer of the scikit-learn and imbalanced-learn libraries.

We discussed:

  • Guillaume's career journey, roles at :probabl. and Inria
  • Overview of scikit-learn, compatible libraries for data preprocessing
  • Development of scikit-learn, focus areas, challenges, and community involvement
  • The role of public feedback, trends, community surveys, and issue/PR management
  • Interaction with key projects like NumPy and SciPy, and the impact of foundational library updates
  • Future directions and use of LLMs in documentation
  • Steps for contributing to Scikit-learn and avoiding redundant efforts
