🔧 Free Workshop: Predicting Financial Time Series
This workshop is part of our free series focused on financial data automation and analysis, led by Ivan Brigida, the creator of the Stock Market Analytics Zoomcamp.

In the first session titled "Economics and Automation Workshop: Building a Data Pipeline for Economic Insights," participants created an automated data pipeline designed to retrieve and analyze macroeconomic indicators.

In our upcoming session, we will cover all aspects of predicting financial time series:
  • ​​Data Acquisition & preprocessing (using Yahoo Finance API, SQLite storage, and exploratory analysis)
  • ​Forecasting techniques (including ARIMA, deep learning, volatility assessments, and confidence intervals)
  • Visualization & deployment (featuring Plotly, Streamlit Cloud, and GitHub Actions)
