How the Homework Assignments Work
The process is simple: solve the tasks locally, publish your solution to a public GitHub repo (or similar), and submit the link through the form.

Each homework comes with a strict deadline listed on the schedule. After the deadline passes, the form closes.

Submissions also appear on the leaderboard.

You can also gain extra points by sharing your learning publicly with the hashtag #mlzoomcamp and tagging Alexey Grigorev or DataTalksClub.

Homework Grade

Homework grade = points for questions + 1 point for the FAQ + a maximum of 7 points for learning in public.

  • Learning in public: up to 7 points, depending on how many platforms you shared your post on (e.g., LinkedIn, X, blog) and the quality of the post.
  • FAQ: worth only 1 point. If you want to get a score for FAQ, contribute to the FAQ repo and add a link to your PR to your homework submission.

Optional questions are scored as well. Please make sure to read the submission form carefully, as all details are mentioned there.

For those who say they weren't graded — this isn't possible. You can log in to check your results, or use CTRL+F to find your name on the leaderboard: https://courses.datatalks.club/ml-zoomcamp-2025/leaderboard.

And just to remind: the homework is for practice. It doesn't affect your certificate.
