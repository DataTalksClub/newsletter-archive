New Podcast Episode: Knowledge Graphs and LLMs Across Academia and Industry

We published a new podcast episode with Anahita Pakiman, a Data scientist-engineer with a strong background in the knowledge graph, semantic web, digitalization, and mechanical engineering.

We discussed:

  • Anahita's Journey: From mechanical engineering to AI, blending automotive industry insights with cognitive sciences.
  • Knowledge Graphs in Automotive: Revolutionizing data management and simulation analysis in the automotive sector.
  • Academic and Industrial Impact: Broadening the application of knowledge graphs across various domains.
  • Predictive Analysis: Leveraging machine learning and knowledge graphs for vehicle performance predictions.
  • LMS Integration: Utilizing Language Models to enrich knowledge graphs for deeper data insights.
  • Adaptive Learning Project: Innovative use of LMS and knowledge graphs to craft dynamic educational content.
  • Development Hurdles: Navigating challenges in web application deployment and complex data visualization.
