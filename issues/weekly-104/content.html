Data Engineering Zoomcamp
Do you want to learn Data Engineering? In 2023, we start another iteration of Data Engineering Zoomcamp. It's a free, practical, 10-week long course about the main concepts in Data Engineering. 

Join us to learn about:
  • Docker, Terraform and GCP
  • Orchestration with Prefect

Podcast
We uploaded a new podcast episode recently From Software Engineer to Data Science Manager with Sadat Anwar
  • Sadat is a data science manager who previously worked as a software engineer
  • Sadat discussed his pivot point as a backend engineer and his exposure to ML and Data Science
  • Sadat talked about his "Act Before you Think" approach to problem solving
  • Sadat discussed his transition into management, including the hiring process and the importance of people management skills
  • Sadat talked about the difficulties of transitioning to management, including the need to focus on projects and setting milestones
  • Sadat discussed how his experience as an engineering manager helped him transition to data science management.
