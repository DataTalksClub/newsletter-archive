Podcast: Building Production Search Systems

We talked with Daniel, an entrepreneurial technologist with over 20 years of experience.

Daniel is a former tech lead for ML infrastructure at YouTube Ads, where his ad performance forecasting engine powers $10B in ad purchases annually.

Now, he is a co-founder of Superlinked, a startup simplifying info-retrieval-heavy systems. They are also the creators of Vectorhub, a free and open-source learning hub for people interested in adding vector retrieval to their ML stack.

We discussed:

  • Search and information retrieval: definitions and methods
  • Vector-based search: a more efficient process
  • Compute problem in vector search
  • Application and implementation of vector-based search systems
  • Vector Hub and Superlink
  • Real-world use cases of vector-based search
  • Future directions and advice
