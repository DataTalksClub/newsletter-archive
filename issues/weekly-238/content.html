A Case Study of ML Zoomcamp Graduate's Final Project

To earn a certificate for ML Zoomcamp, students create an end-to-end final project that turns course concepts into a working system.

We recently met with Serena Haidar, one of the ML Zoomcamp graduates, who walked us through her final project – building a waste classifier.

In her article, she shared the process behind this project from training an Xception-based image classifier on ~15,000 waste images to serving predictions via a Flask API packaged in Docker.

Learn how she moves from a notebook to a small production app.
