Podcast
Listen to our new podcast episode about Navigating Career Changes in Machine Learning with Krzysztof Szafanek.

Krzysztof is a seasoned engineer with 17 years of professional experience in building software. Krzysztof supports machine learning pipelines as an ML Platform engineer and internal consultant.

We discussed Krzysztof's background, different aspects of being a generalist, and the importance of learning things you're interested in.

Outline of our talk:
  • Switching careers multiple times, freedom at companies, sabbatical
  • Being a generalist: pros and cons, specifics of the job market 
  • Tips to enjoy learning and book recommendations from Krzysztof
