Book of the Week

Ask your questions actively on our Slack community and get one of the five free copies of the book!

📕 The book of this week is Snowflake: The Definitive Guide by Joyce Kay Avila.
Snowflake is a full SQL data warehouse built from the ground up for the cloud. You can eliminate data silos and run workloads from a single platform with Snowflake.

In this book, you’ll learn how to build modern integrated data applications. Using hands-on SQL examples, you’ll also discover how the Snowflake Data Cloud helps you accelerate data science.
To participate:
  • Go to the #book-of-the-week channel in our Slack
  • Ask as many questions as you’d like
  • The book authors answer questions from Monday to Thursday
  • On Friday, the authors will choose the winners of their free ebook copies by selecting the best questions

Open-Source Spotlight
Open-Source Spotlight is our series where we're discovering open-source tools and recording a short demo of the tool. This time, it was Ultralytics YOLOv8 with Glenn Jocher.

YOLOv8 is an object detection algorithm that divides images into a grid system.

Our demo includes these steps
  1. Set up your environment
  2. Validate your model
  3. Train your model
  4. Export your model
  5. Python Usage
  6. Detection, Segmentation, Classification
