Podcast
Our new podcast episode on Doing Software Engineering in Academia is out!

This time, our guest is Johanna Bayer. Johanna does a Ph.D. in machine learning in clinical neuroimaging and advocates for open source and open science.

We talked about research software engineering and its current state in Academia.
  • What is research software engineering?
  • How do one does research software engineering?
  • Are research software engineering and Academia compatible?
We discussed Johanna's background and study process.
  • What are the effective ways of learning software engineering skills?
  • How often does Johanna do hackathons, open source, and freelancing?
Through our talk, we were exploring different aspects of research software engineering and the efforts of Johanna and other enthusiasts in open source and open science.
